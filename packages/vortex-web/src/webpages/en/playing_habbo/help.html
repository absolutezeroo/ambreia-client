How to deal with a problem in Vortex

You may occasionally come across the odd misbehaving Vortex. Never fear, help is at hand! On this page we'll tell you what tools work best in tricky situations.

In a room

When you're in a room and another Vortex is being out-of-line, click on their avatar and a drop-down menu will appear. This lets you either ignore, moderate or in really disruptive cases, report them.

Ignoring a Vortex

If a Vortex is saying things which make you feel uncomfortable, you can put them on ignore. This is an ideal solution for teasing, spamming or when you simply want to say "Bye Felicia!" but don't know how.

  1. Click on their avatar. A drop-down menu will appear.
  2. Choose the Ignore option.
  3. You won't see what the Vortex says anymore. If you want to un-ignore later, click the avatar again and choose Listen.

Moderating a Vortex

In your own rooms, or if you have room rights, you can decide who can visit the room, and you have the power to mute, kick or ban other users. This allows you to play an active part in the general Vortex moderation and to contribute to a safer and more enjoyable community.

Read more on Vortex Helpdesk about room moderation tools and room settings.

Reporting a Vortex

If things are getting heated in a room: Vortexs are talking about meeting in real life, want to video call, exchange personal contact details or someone's being badly bullied… consider reporting that person. No one likes a tattle-tale, so remember to use this only when someone is intentionally doing harm to others or themselves.

  1. Click on the trouble-making Vortex's avatar. A drop-down menu will appear.
  2. Click the avatar name.
  3. Click the reporting flag icon.
  4. Choose a topic for your call that best matches your problem.
  5. Tell our moderation team what happened.
  6. Send the help request and our moderation team will try to resolve the issue. If you chose Bullying a Guardian may intervene.

In personal messaging/chat

If you are chatting to someone on PM and they are making you feel uncomfortable:

  1. Click the Report button below the other Vortex's picture in the chat window.
  2. You will be asked for more information about what happened.
  3. Our moderation team will then take appropriate action.

In a group forum

You can report an inappropriate group forum thread or post:

  1. Click the orange flag icon in the forum.
  2. You will be asked for more information about the situation.
  3. Our moderation team will then take appropriate action.

On a web page

You can report an inappropriate photo, room or room image on a room homepage or photo page:

  1. Click the white flag icon
  2. Choose a topic for your call that best matches your problem
  3. Let us know what's wrong with the room or photo
  4. Our moderation team will then take appropriate action
