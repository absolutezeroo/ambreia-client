Vortex text lab — Flash parity

Ways of putting the same string on a canvas, compared pixel by pixel against a capture of the real Flash client. The number that matters is MAE: mean absolute coverage error per pixel, after aligning both on their ink bounding box. A signature (share of partly-covered pixels) is shown too, but on its own it is misleading — Chrome's fillText pushes coverage toward the extremes, so it can score "less anti-aliased" than Flash while looking blurrier, because its spread lands below the threshold rather than above it.
